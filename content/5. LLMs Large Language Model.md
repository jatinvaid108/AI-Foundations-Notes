## 🧠 **How LLMs Work (Simple Notes)**

- **LLMs (Large Language Models)** are a type of **Generative AI**.
    
- They are trained to **understand and generate text** — like ChatGPT writing answers or stories.
    
- They are built using **Deep Learning** and **Neural Networks** (specifically a deep architecture called a _Transformer_ — you’ll learn that later).
    

---

## ⚙️ **How They Are Trained**

1. **Training Data:**
    
    - They are trained on huge text datasets — books, websites, articles, conversations, etc.
        
    - The model learns **patterns of words**, grammar, facts, and reasoning.
        
2. **Learning Process:**
    
    - It sees millions of sentences like:  
        “The cat is on the ___”  
        and learns that “mat” is the most likely next word.
        
    - Repeating this billions of times helps it understand **context and language flow**.
        
3. **Neural Network Role:**
    
    - The model has **neurons (nodes)** arranged in layers (like human brain connections).
        
    - Each neuron adjusts its strength (weights) to improve predictions during training.
        
4. **Training Goal:**
    
    - The model tries to **predict the next word** in a sentence correctly.
        
    - Errors are reduced step-by-step through a process called **backpropagation**.
        
5. **Inference (Using the Model):**
    
    - Once trained, when you give input like  
        “Write a poem about the moon,”  
        the model uses what it learned to **generate new, meaningful text**.
        

---

### 🧩 In short

> **LLMs = Deep Learning Models trained on huge text data to predict and generate human-like language.**
