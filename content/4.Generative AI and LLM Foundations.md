**Machine Learning (ML):**  
ML is a subset of AI that enables systems to **learn patterns from data** and make predictions or decisions without being explicitly programmed.

**Deep Learning (DL):**  
DL is a subset of ML that uses **Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs)** with many layers to **automatically learn complex patterns** — such as identifying images, speech, or music — from large amounts of data.

## **Generative AI (Gen AI)**

### 🔹 What is Generative AI?

Generative AI is a branch of Artificial Intelligence that focuses on **creating new data or content** — such as **text, images, music, or videos** — that resembles the data it was trained on.  
It doesn’t just classify or predict; it **learns patterns** in data and then **generates new, original outputs** based on those patterns.

### 🔹 How Generative AI Works

1. **Pattern Learning (Training Phase):**  
    The model studies large datasets (e.g., millions of images, songs, or text).  
    It learns **how the data is structured** — shapes, colors, words, tones, or melodies.
    
2. **Pattern Generation (Inference Phase):**  
    Once trained, it uses that learned knowledge to **create something new**, not just reproduce old data.
    
    - It doesn’t copy existing images but **generates fresh ones** with similar characteristics.
        
    - It predicts what should come next — like the next pixel, word, or note — based on patterns it learned.
        

---

### 🔹 Relation Between ML and Gen AI

|Feature|**Machine Learning (ML)**|**Generative AI (Gen AI)**|
|---|---|---|
|**Goal**|Learn from data to make **predictions/classifications**|Learn from data to **create new content**|
|**Input**|Data + Labels (e.g., image of dog labeled "dog")|Large unlabeled data (e.g., millions of dog images or songs)|
|**Output**|Label or value (e.g., “dog”)|New data (e.g., a new dog image, new melody, new paragraph)|
|**Example**|Predict if an image is a cat or dog|Generate a new image of a cat that never existed|
|**Model Type**|Regression, Classification models|Diffusion Models, GANs, Transformers|

---

### 🔹 Example Flow

#### 🧠 Machine Learning Example:

- **Training:** Model learns to classify photos of cats vs. dogs using labeled datasets.
    
- **Inference:** Given a new photo, model predicts whether it’s a cat or dog.
    

#### 🎨 Generative AI Example:

- **Training:** Model studies thousands of cat and dog photos to learn visual features like fur texture, eyes, shape, etc.
    
- **Inference:** Model generates a **completely new cat image** that looks realistic but doesn’t exist in reality.
    

---

### 🔹 Types of Generative AI Models

1. **Text-based Models (Language Models):**
    
    - Examples: ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude
        
    - Trained on massive text data; generate new text, answers, or stories.
        
2. **Image-based Models:**
    
    - Examples: DALL·E, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion
        
    - Trained on image-text pairs; generate new images from text prompts.
        
3. **Audio/Music-based Models:**
    
    - Examples: Suno, Mubert, Jukebox
        
    - Learn musical patterns (rhythm, tone, melody) and compose **original songs**.
        
4. **Multimodal Models:**
    
    - Understand and generate across multiple formats — **text, image, video, audio**.
        
    - Example: **GPT-4**, **Gemini**, **Sora (video model)**.
        

---

### 🔹 Summary

- **ML:** Learns → Predicts (e.g., label/class)
    
- **Gen AI:** Learns → **Creates** (new, original content)
    
- **ML output = classification or regression result**
    
- **Gen AI output = generated data (text/image/music)**
